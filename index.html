TOWN

A place for better conversations.

Town is a community discussion platform built around downtowns: spaces for shared interests, local topics, questions, and conversations.

How Town works

Explore public downtowns, read published discussions, ask questions, and take part in conversations with other members.

  • Explore downtowns: Find communities organised around topics and shared interests.
  • Browse discussions: Read public posts and follow conversations across communities.
  • Choose your feed: Use available sorting controls, including Latest and Most Discussed, to decide how discussions are ordered.
  • Contribute: Create an account when you are ready to join communities, publish posts, comment, or vote.
